Bundled Python search runtime
=============================

The browser resource bundle includes unmodified files from these projects:

Pyodide 314.0.8, Mozilla Public License 2.0
  License: Pyodide-MPL-2.0.txt
  Source: https://github.com/pyodide/pyodide/tree/314.0.8
  Distribution: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/pyodide/v314.0.8/full/

CPython 3.14.2, Python Software Foundation License and bundled notices
  License: CPython-LICENSE.txt
  Source: https://github.com/python/cpython/tree/v3.14.2

NumPy 2.4.6, BSD-3-Clause and bundled third-party notices
  Licenses: NumPy-licenses.txt (collected from the unchanged wheel)
  Source: https://github.com/numpy/numpy/tree/v2.4.6

The original NumPy wheel, including its license files, remains intact in the
resource bundle. The sibling pyodide-resources-manifest.json records the exact
source URLs, byte sizes, and SHA-256 hashes of all six bundled resources.

The homepage's loader and DTmu adapter are separate from the runtime modules.
